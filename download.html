How your licence key arrives

  1. Buy Warrior Tax Pro Elite — Professional ($999) or Professional Plus ($1,299) on our pricing page. Professional Plus adds business returns (1120, 1120-S and 1065).
  2. We email your licence key to the address you used at checkout within 1 business day of purchase, with the installation guide.
  3. When the installer is released, we email your download link to the same address. Install the program, and enter your licence key and EFIN when it asks.
Warrior Tax Pro Online ($799) is a web account at warriortaxpro.com. It needs no download and has no desktop licence key.

Need a new download link?

A download link works for 7 days and 5 downloads. For a new one, enter your licence key. We email the link to the address on your licence; it is never shown here.

Check that the installer is genuine

Before you open the installer, right-click it, choose Properties, then the Digital Signatures tab. The signer must be Warrior Financial Services LLC, and its Details must say "This digital signature is OK." When you install, Windows names the same publisher.

If the signature names anyone else, or Windows says "Unknown publisher", do not run the file. Contact us.
