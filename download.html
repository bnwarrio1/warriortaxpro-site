System requirements

WindowsA 64-bit Windows PC. Windows 11 is recommended. Windows 10 (64-bit) runs the program, but Microsoft stopped security updates for Windows 10 on October 14, 2025, unless the PC is enrolled in Extended Security Updates, and a computer that holds taxpayer information should get security updates.
Disk spaceAbout 560 MB for the program, plus room for your returns, client documents and backups.
InternetNeeded to activate your licence, to receive updates and to e-file.
Windows administratorNeeded to install the program and to install every update. If you sign in to Windows as a standard user, someone with administrator rights (the computer's owner or your IT person) types their name and password when Windows asks.
Your EFINYour 6-digit IRS Electronic Filing Identification Number, asked for when you activate.
A phone with an authenticator appFor two-step sign-in, for example Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator. Text-message codes are offered too.

How your licence key arrives

  1. Buy Warrior Tax Pro Elite — Professional ($999) or Professional Plus ($1,299) on our pricing page. Professional Plus adds business returns (1120, 1120-S and 1065).
  2. We email your licence key to the address you used at checkout within 1 business day of purchase, with the installation guide.
  3. From November 16, download the installer here (or from that email), install it, and enter your licence key and EFIN when the program asks.
Warrior Tax Pro Online ($799) is a web account at warriortaxpro.com. It needs no download and has no desktop licence key.

Check that the installer is genuine

Before you open the installer, right-click it, choose Properties, then the Digital Signatures tab. The signer must be Warrior Financial Services LLC, and its Details must say "This digital signature is OK." When you install, Windows names the same publisher.

If the signature names anyone else, or Windows says "Unknown publisher", do not run the file. Contact us.
