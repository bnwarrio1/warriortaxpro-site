## Getting started

1. **Download** - the installer is available from **November 16, 2026**:
   {{DOWNLOAD_LINK - live from Nov 16 only; until then: "We will email you the download link on

2. **Check it is genuine** - right-click the file, choose **Properties**, then **Digital
   Signatures**. It must be signed by **Warrior Financial Services LLC**.
3. **Install** - Windows asks a **Windows administrator** to approve the install, and every update

   licence key and EFIN.

6. **Sign in** - create your administrator account, then set up two-step sign-in with an
   authenticator app, and **save your recovery codes**.

- An internet connection, to activate, receive updates and e-file.
- A phone with an authenticator app (or text messages) for two-step sign-in.
